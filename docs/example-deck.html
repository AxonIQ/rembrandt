Q3 numbers · Acme All-Hands · #07

The migration gap

Signups are up 157% and clusters up 177%. Production migrations went the other way. The rate at which a cluster leaves Postgres and becomes a production workload fell from 5.8% to 1.9%. That is a friction problem, not a demand problem.

Funnel, Q1 → Q3 2026Q1Q3Change
Free signups self-serve accounts6,74217,318+157%
Clusters created any size, any region2,4896,904+177%
Production migrations off Postgres143128-10%
Cluster → production migration rate5.75%1.85%-68%

At Q1’s migration rate, 6,904 clusters would have produced about 397 production workloads, roughly $56M of committed ARR at a $141K enterprise ACV. We have 128, worth $18.1M.

What each side owes the other

Three obligations that belong to a team, and one that belongs to everyone.

GTM
Build the pipeline, and close what we build. Bring the regional committee into the room early, not at the security review.
Operations
Keep the plan honest and the team whole. Say the number out loud in the week it moves, not the month after.
Engineering
Ship the migration path and cut the friction. Every week a trial stalls is a week the default wins by doing nothing.

Licensing · Acme All-Hands · #18

How we got here

Four facts about the license that have been true since the first release.

01

Never permissive

The engine has never shipped under a BSD or MIT-style license.

02

Source available

The core has always been source available, with a commercial grant layered on top.

03

90 days notice

Tier changes take effect 90 days after written notice, and not before.

04

Node and seat caps

The community grant covers development and a single node. It always has.

Licensing · Acme All-Hands · #20

The two-signal rule

Both have to be true before anyone gates an account.

Over the caps for two full quarters

The overage held across two consecutive billing periods. A single spike during a load test is not a signal.

Signal 01

Confirmed in two independent places

Control plane telemetry and registry pulls both show the same footprint, at the same scale.

Signal 02
Sustained overage only. This is not a default posture, and it is not a sales tactic.
Licensing · Acme All-Hands · #21

If a customer raises licensing

The same two rules, whoever asks and however they ask it.

Do
Bring in Elena, Sam and Legal
All three, every time. If licensing or compliance comes up in any conversation, loop them in before you answer anything.
Stay level. Keep your tone measured, whatever the tone coming the other way.
Don’t
Answer it yourself
Not to the customer, not in the deal room, not in a channel with a hot take attached.
Route it to Elena, Sam and Legal first, then let them decide who replies.
